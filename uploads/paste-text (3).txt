What DishBar Is

a restaurant
a meal prep company

DishBar IS:

A trusted marketplace connecting local home chefs with customers looking for authentic homemade meals.

DishBar creates economic opportunities for home chefs while helping customers discover fresh homemade food prepared by trusted people in their communities.

Every design decision must reinforce this message.

Your Mission

Forget how the application looks today.

Audit every page and redesign the product experience from first principles.

Ask yourself continuously:

"If I landed on this page for the first time, would I immediately understand that DishBar connects people—not sells food?"

If the answer is no, redesign it.

AI Audit

After redesigning every screen, review the entire platform again.

List:

Every confusing page.

Every weak headline.

Every unnecessary feature.

Every missing trust element.

Every conversion improvement.

Implement all high-impact improvements before stopping.

Do not simply make the website prettier.

Transform DishBar into the most trustworthy home-chef marketplace in Canada.

Then I would immediately follow it with a second prompt.

This is the prompt I think will add the most business value:

Act as an experienced marketplace founder who has launched platforms like Airbnb, Etsy, Uber, and Fiverr. Audit DishBar from the perspective of both a first-time home chef and a first-time customer. Identify every point where users might hesitate, become confused, or abandon the platform. Redesign those experiences to maximize trust, simplicity, and conversion. Don't add unnecessary features. Remove friction. Every change must increase the likelihood that a visitor becomes either a registered chef or a paying customer. Finish by prioritizing improvements by estimated impact on business growth.

One thing I noticed from your current preview

Your hero still says:

"Authentic Persian Home Cooking, Delivered"

That headline no longer matches the company you're building.

If I were a home cook looking to earn money, I wouldn't realize the platform was for me.

If I had to pick one single change that would increase your chances of success before launch, it would be changing the homepage so that every visitor immediately sees the two clear paths:

🍳 Become a Home Chef
🍽️ Find Homemade Meals

That one decision aligns the entire product with your actual business model and gives both sides of the marketplace a clear starting point.
